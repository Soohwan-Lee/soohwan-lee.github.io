## 👋 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. candidate** in the [Expressive Computing Lab](https://expc.unist.ac.kr/) at the [Department of Design](https://design.unist.ac.kr/), [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr/), working in **Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Human-centered AI**. *(Ph.D. expected 2027)*

Most AI research studies one user talking to one system. I study what happens when **AI joins a group** — how it shifts decisions, influence, and power among people who must decide together.

---

## 🔬 Research

Drawing on social psychological theory, I design and empirically study AI systems that intervene in group processes. I advance **Group-Centered AI**, treating user experience as collective and emergent rather than a sum of individual interactions — building AI that does not merely optimize group outcomes, but helps people **see how those outcomes emerge**.

| Theme | What I study |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI Social Influence** | How AI agents exert normative and informative influence — and when people comply, convert, or resist |
| **Group-centered AI & GX** | Frameworks extending Human-centered AI beyond the individual to **Group Experience** |
| **Minority Support** | LLM-powered counterargument and mediation that protect minority views in power-imbalanced groups |
| **Reflective Interfaces** | Visualizations surfacing value conflicts, participation gaps, and influence trajectories |
